How it works

No new phone number to give out, no app for your customers to download. It runs quietly behind the calls you're already getting.

01

A call goes unanswered

Your business line forwards a missed call to us. Nobody is billed for the call — it's never actually connected.

02

We text them back, instantly

Within seconds, the caller gets a text so they know they've been heard instead of wondering if anyone will call back.

03

The conversation continues

Depending on your plan, that's either a short standard message or an ongoing reply from your AI receptionist.

Two ways to respond

Pick what fits how your business runs. Either way, the response goes out the moment a call is missed.

Static Reply

One consistent, dependable message, sent every time.

  • Instant text-back after a missed call
  • A single message you write once
  • Simple to set up and simple to trust
AI Receptionist

A real back-and-forth conversation, handled for you.

  • Everything in Static Reply
  • Answers follow-up questions in real time
  • You get a summary once the conversation wraps up
